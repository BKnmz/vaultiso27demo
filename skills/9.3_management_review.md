The document must define:
1. Frequency and scheduling of management reviews (at minimum annually)
2. Required inputs to the review (must include all mandatory ISO 27001 inputs)
3. Required outputs and decisions from the review
4. Who must attend
5. A reusable agenda template for management review meetings
6. How review outputs are documented and actioned

Mandatory review inputs per ISO 27001:2022 clause 9.3.2:
- Status of previous review actions
- Changes in external/internal issues
- IS performance and effectiveness (nonconformities, monitoring results, audit results)
- Feedback from interested parties
- Risk assessment results and risk treatment plan status
- Opportunities for continual improvement
